## Two example systems for the whole course

Instead of inventing a new example every time, this course keeps coming back to two fixed systems:

### 1. ATM

Everyone has used an ATM — which makes it easy to talk about. We already know what capabilities it has, who its users are, and how we expect it to behave.

### 2. Tree-seed drone

An unmanned aerial vehicle that flies out to areas where trees have been cut down and access for replanting is difficult. This drone is loaded with tree seeds and drops them into the deforested zone.

## Why these two?

Both the ATM and the tree-seed drone are reasonably complex systems that have both **hardware** and **software** — so we can use them to illustrate every category of requirement this course covers:

- Functional requirements
- Non-functional/quality requirements
- User interface requirements
- Business requirements
- Statement of work requirements
- Interface requirements

Whenever this course introduces a new concept, we'll usually start with the ATM (simpler, more familiar), and sometimes lean on the other example to illustrate a more complex idea (like system boundaries or the operational environment).
